01 · Abstract

Camera moves. The world holds.

Precise camera control is essential for cinematic character video generation, yet existing methods struggle to follow large-angle trajectories while preserving character identity across viewpoint changes. We introduce CLACT, a character-centric benchmark of 80,000 training samples and 42 evaluation scenes, and WorldCast, a framework that couples scale-aligned geometric guidance with geometry-aligned multi-view character memory (GAMA). WorldCast achieves the best camera-control accuracy and character consistency while maintaining competitive visual quality.

Figure 1 · Large-angle trajectories that break prior methods — best camera accuracy (ATE ↓), cross-view consistency (MEt3R ↓), and identity preservation, at competitive image quality.

DATASET
COMPONENT 01

CLACT Benchmark

A character-centric dataset & benchmark for Large-Angle Camera Trajectories: 80,000 synthetic training samples (50K UE5 + 30K Blender), 42 synthetic and real-world evaluation scenes, accurate camera geometry, and paired multi-view character observations. Dataset · coming soon

GUIDANCE
COMPONENT 02

Scale-Aligned Geometric Guidance

The initial view is lifted to a colored 3D point cloud with calibrated depth scale, then splat-rendered along the target trajectory — producing trajectory-aligned RGB guidance and coverage masks that tell the model exactly what to complete.

MEMORY
COMPONENT 03

Geometry-Aligned Memory Attention

Multi-view anchor geometry is aligned with the target trajectory in a shared coordinate frame via Umeyama alignment, enabling geometry-aware retrieval and injection of view-relevant character features into video tokens.
